📖 How to Play Poker Duel

1. Starting Chips & Blinds: Both players start with $1,000. Blinds escalate every 3 rounds.

2. Hole Cards: Each player gets 3 private cards.

3. Keep / Discard Drafting: Cards are drawn from the deck one at a time. The active player chooses to KEEP (locks card into 1 of the 5 community spaces) or DISCARD. Drafting alternates until all 5 spaces are filled!

4. Yellow Hand Assist Bar: The yellow HUD meter dynamically evaluates your best 5-card poker combination in real-time as cards are dealt or kept.

5. Showdown: Best 5 cards out of your 3 hole cards + 5 table cards wins the pot!
